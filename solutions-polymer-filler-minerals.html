Industrial buyer depth

Commercial qualification notes for polymer minerals

Element keeps the original page framework while adding more buyer-facing detail for international procurement teams. The notes below are intended to guide early screening before the exact grade, TDS, SDS and COA package is confirmed.

Relevant mineral routemineral fillers for plastics and polymer compounds.
Buyer performance questionsTarget application, required whiteness or colour profile, particle size range, dispersion behaviour, moisture tolerance, formulation constraints and annual demand.
Specification checkpointsTypical review points include brightness or whiteness, particle size distribution, residue, moisture, LOI, pH, oil absorption, bulk density and application-specific purity requirements.
Application fitCommon review areas include coatings, ceramics, polymers, paper, rubber, construction materials and specialised mineral formulations, depending on the grade selected.
Packaging route25 kg bags, jumbo bags and bulk shipment routes can be reviewed according to trial order size, destination port and handling requirements.
Documents availableTDS, SDS, COA, packing list, commercial invoice and third-party test documents can be aligned during buyer qualification.
